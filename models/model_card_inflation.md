# Model card: Canadian inflation forecaster

Generated by `scripts/train_inflation.py` on 2026-11-14. Data retrieved on 2026-11-14 from: Bank of Canada Valet API, FRED, Federal Reserve Bank of St. Louis.

## What it predicts

Change in Canada total CPI y/y inflation (percentage points) over h months, for horizons of 3, 6, 12 months, from the latest published month of Canadian CPI.

## Methods compared

| Method | Description |
|---|---|
| `ridge` | ridge regression on macro features |
| `mean_reversion` | partial return toward the 2% target (one coefficient fitted on past data) |
| `random_walk` | no change from today's inflation |
| `target_2pct` | the Bank of Canada's 2% target |

Ridge features and why they are included:

| Feature | Reason |
|---|---|
| `yoy` | Current inflation: inflation is persistent. |
| `core_yoy` | Core inflation (CPIW): the underlying trend, less affected by volatile items. |
| `accel_3m` | Change in y/y inflation over the last 3 months: momentum. |
| `oil_yoy` | Crude oil (WTI) y/y change: energy prices pass quickly into CPI. |
| `fx_yoy` | USD/CAD y/y change: a weaker dollar raises import prices. |
| `yield_2y` | 2-year Government of Canada yield: market view of the policy path. |
| `slope_10y_2y` | 10-year minus 2-year yield: growth expectations. |
| `us_yoy` | U.S. CPI inflation: common global inflation pressures. |
| `us_unrate_chg` | 12-month change in U.S. unemployment: economic slack. |

## Findings and limits

- The ridge model on macro features does not beat simple rules out of sample. The method used is the one with the lowest walk-forward error, and the application says which one it is.
- Errors are much larger since 2020 (pandemic and the 2021-2023 inflation surge) than in 2008-2019; intervals are built from past errors and widen accordingly.
- Macro series from FRED are latest revised values, not the values first published; Canadian CPI is not revised, so the target itself is unaffected.
- Forecasts inform scenarios and real-return estimates. They are not used to time markets.
